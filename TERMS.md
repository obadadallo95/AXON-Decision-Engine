Welcome to the AXON Decision Engine challenge prototype. This document is a project disclaimer, not a production SLA, security certification, or enterprise contract.

## 1. Scope of Use

AXON evaluates AI-proposed actions against typed, server-owned challenge policies.
- **Canonical states**: `EXECUTE`, `ASK`, `DEFER`, `ESCALATE`, and `REFUSE` are the primary model. Legacy four-state projections remain API compatibility fields only.
- **No direct execution**: AXON does not execute real refunds, deployments, or tool actions. Any external gateway must enforce the returned decision.
- **Prototype data**: Evidence is synthetic, reviewer identity is simulated, and audit storage is process-local.

---

## 2. Technical Grounding & AI Models

AXON may use Google Gemini for bounded, optional advisory interpretation. Gemini is not authoritative, and the project does not claim external search grounding, perfect accuracy, or verified evidence acquisition.

## 3. Policy boundary

The challenge scenario runner uses server-owned policy sets. Caller-supplied policy prose cannot grant authorization, and the visible policy editor is not part of the judge-facing flow.
